For the same 6 assays, the mean Spearman change at 50% requested coverage before matching was +0.0322. The 95% range of changes from random retention was [-0.0127, +0.0137] before matching and [-0.0228, +0.0210] after matching. The paired interval includes zero, so a change in the advantage after matching was not clearly established.
